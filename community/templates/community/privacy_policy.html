Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

WomenKonnect ("we", "us", "our") operates womenkonnect.co.in (the "Site"), a community platform where women can share stories, ask questions, and connect anonymously and safely. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: When you register, we collect your username, email address, and password (stored securely, encrypted). You may optionally add a profile photo and bio.

Content you post: Posts, replies, likes, and any other content you choose to share on the Site.

Usage data: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Site — pages viewed, time spent, general location (country/city level), and device/browser type. This data is aggregated and does not personally identify you.

Cookies: We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and measure site traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Site
  • To let you create posts, reply, and interact with other members
  • To send account-related emails (activation, password reset, notifications)
  • To understand aggregate usage trends and improve the experience
  • To keep the community safe (e.g. reviewing reported content)

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Google Analytics — website traffic analysis (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (account activation, password resets)
  • Supabase — secure database hosting
  • Render — website hosting infrastructure

We may in the future display advertisements through Google AdSense. If enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Anonymity & Safety

WomenKonnect is built as a safe space. We do not publicly display your email address, and posts show only your chosen username. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Your Rights

You can update or delete your profile information at any time by logging in. To request deletion of your account and associated data, or if you have any privacy questions, contact us at support@womenkonnect.co.in.

6. Children's Privacy

WomenKonnect is intended for adult women and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encrypted password storage and secure hosting. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, reach us at support@womenkonnect.co.in.
